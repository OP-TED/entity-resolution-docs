== UC-W4  Consult Resolution Statistics

*Use Case Level:* White (Summary) +
*Primary Actor:* Curator (human user) +
*Scope:* Link Curation Application and ERS +
*Stakeholders and Interests*

* Curators require a concise and reliable overview of current clustering activity in order to prioritise review efforts and understand operational workload.
*Brief Description* +
This use case describes how a Curator consults aggregated, read-only statistics about the current state of entity resolution. +
The statistics provide high-level indicators derived from the current operational state of the system. They are intended for situational awareness and workload prioritisation, not for auditing or governance control. +
Statistics may include, per entity type and optionally per time window:

* total number of Entity Mentions,
* total number of canonical clusters (cluster identifiers) in the decissions store,
* number of recent resolution requests (e.g last day),
All statistics are aggregated counts. No individual Entity Mention, cluster, or decision details are exposed through this use case. +
The operation is strictly read-only. It does not trigger resolution, re-evaluation, reclustering, or any modification of system state. +
*Goal / Value* +
Provide an aggregated and non-intrusive view of clustering and re-evaluation activity so that Curators can assess workload and operational dynamics without inspecting individual records. +
*Scope (System-of-Interest)* +
The Link Curation Application and ERSys, limited to retrieval and presentation of aggregated statistics derived from the Decision Store and related operational metadata. +
*Out of Scope*

* Inspection or modification of individual Entity Mentions or Cluster Assignments.
* Operational performance monitoring and specific metrics.
* Historical trend analysis beyond simple timeframe filtering.
* Administrative or corrective actions.
*Trigger* +
A Curator works with the Link Curation app which always shows resolution statistics. +
*Preconditions*

* The Curator is authenticated and authorised to access statistical views.
* The statistics interface is available.
*Success Guarantees (Postconditions)*

* Aggregated statistics are presented to the Curator, optionally scoped by entity type and timeframe.
* Statistics reflect the observable state of the system at the time of retrieval.
* No system state is modified.
*Minimal Guarantees (On Failure)*

* No system state is modified.
* The Curator is informed that statistics are temporarily unavailable.
*Notes / Business Rules*

* Statistics are derived from existing operational stores .
* Counts are indicative and may change as resolution, re-evaluation, and reclustering activities continue.
* Statistics may temporarily reflect transitional states during high-volume reclustering operations.
* Statistics are intended for operational awareness and must not be interpreted as audit or compliance artefacts.
*Frequency of Occurrence* +
Low; typically consulted during curation an review.
