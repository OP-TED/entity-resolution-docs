= Decisions Queue
:description: How to review, accept, reject, and select alternative clusters for resolution decisions in the Curation Web Application.

The *Decisions* page is the primary workspace.
It lists resolution decisions that require manual review and lets you record your feedback on each proposed match.

image::curation/full-decisions-page.png[Full Decisions page]

== User Interface Overview

The page is divided into three main sections:

* *Left* — the Decision List, showing all pending decisions matching the active filters.
* *Center* — the Decision Area, showing the entity mention being resolved and the top candidate cluster proposed by the engine.
* *Top* — a bar for filtering and ordering the Decision List.

=== Decision List

The left panel lists all decisions matching the active filters.
Each item shows the entity name, its confidence and similarity scores (colour-coded), and when the decision was created.

Select any item to load its detail in the right panel.

image::curation/decision-list.png[Decision list with score badges]

Score colour coding:

[cols="1,1"]
|===
| Range | Colour

| 0.00 – 0.39 (Low)
| 🔴 Red

| 0.40 – 0.69 (Medium)
| 🟡 Orange/yellow

| 0.70 – 1.00 (High)
| 🟢 Green
|===


=== Filtering and Ordering Bar

The filter bar lets you narrow down the decision list by score range, full-text search, and sort order.
All filters can be combined and take effect instantly.

image::curation/filter-bar.png[Filter bar with Confidence and Similarity dropdowns]

[cols="1,3"]
|===
| Filter | Description

| *Confidence*
| Restrict by confidence score range.
Select a predefined band (Low / Medium / High) or set custom min/max values.
By default the list shows decisions below the system's curation confidence threshold.

| *Similarity*
| Restrict by similarity score range.
Select a predefined band or set custom min/max values.

| *Sort by*
| Order the decision list.
Options: _Created At (Newest/Oldest)_, _Updated At (Newest/Oldest)_, _Confidence (Low to High / High to Low)_.

| *Search*
| Full-text search across entity mention field data (name, identifiers, and other parsed attributes).
|===


=== Decision Area

The Decision Area displays the full details of a selected decision: the entity mention, the proposed cluster match with visual comparison aids, and a list of alternative candidates.
It is also where the curator records their feedback.

image::curation/decision-area.png[Decision area with comparison panel and alternative clusters]

[[alternative-clusters]]
=== Alternative Clusters

Below the comparison cards, the page lists the other candidate clusters that the engine considered — ranked from best to worst after the top proposal.

Expand any cluster to compare its entities against the current entity mention, the same way as the proposed match.

image::curation/alternative-clusters.png[Alternative clusters section with expanded panel]


== Actions

This section provides details on available curation actions and explains how to perform them using the user interface.

=== Reviewing a Decision
The first step in the curation workflow is to review the proposed match for a given entity mention.

Selecting a decision from the Decision List opens the comparison view in the Decision Area.

In this view, you can inspect the details of the entity mention and the proposed cluster together with its member entities to determine if they are a correct match.

The main area is a comparison panel that highlights the differences between the entity mention and an entity in the proposed cluster.
It shows two cards side by side:

* *Left* — the entity mention being resolved, with its field values.
* *Right* — entities of the proposed cluster, with fields that differ from the current entity highlighted.

Use the *Previous* / *Next* controls on the right card to browse other entities within the proposed cluster.

The comparison panel shows three types of differences between the entity mention and the currently selected cluster entity:

* *Added* (green highlight) — field values that are present in the cluster entity but not in the entity mention.
* *Removed* (red highlight) — field values that are present in the entity mention but not in the cluster entity.
* *Changed* (yellow highlight) — field values that are present in both the entity mention and the cluster entity but have different values.

The confidence (*C:*) and similarity (*S:*) scores for this decision are shown in the header area.

image::curation/comparison-panel.png[Comparison panel with entity cards and diff highlighting]

=== Accepting a Decision

Accepting a decision registers your feedback that the proposed cluster is a correct match for this entity mention.

. Select the decision in the list.
. Click the green *✓* button, **or** press kbd:[A].
. Confirm the action in the dialog that appears.
. The decision is removed from the queue and your feedback is recorded in the action history.

[WARNING]
====
Curatorial actions are final and cannot be undone.
Past actions remain visible in the xref:UserGuide/curation-guide-history.adoc[Action History].
====

NOTE: The decision is not immediately accepted in the system — your feedback is stored and used by the engine when re-evaluating future decisions, which may lead to this decision being accepted later on.


=== Rejecting a Decision

Rejecting a decision registers your feedback that none of the proposed candidates is an acceptable match.

. Select the decision in the list.
. Click the red *✗* button, **or** press kbd:[R].
. Confirm the action in the dialog that appears.
. The decision is removed from the queue and your feedback is recorded in the action history.

NOTE: The decision is not immediately rejected in the system — your feedback is stored and used by the engine when re-evaluating future decisions.

=== Selecting an Alternative Cluster

Access the alternative clusters in the lower portion of the Decision Area as described in <<alternative-clusters>>.

If one of the alternatives is a better match than the top proposal:

. Expand the cluster and inspect its entities.
. Click *Use this cluster instead*.
. Confirm the action in the dialog that appears.
. The decision is removed from the queue and your feedback is recorded in the action history.

Click *Load More* to fetch additional alternative clusters beyond those initially shown.
