= Decisions Queue
:description: How to review, accept, reject, and select alternative clusters for resolution decisions in the Curation Web Application.

The *Decisions* page is the primary workspace.
It lists resolution decisions that require manual review and lets you record your feedback on each proposed match.

image::curation/full-decisions-page.png[Full Decisions page]

== User Interface Overview

The page is divided into three main sections:

* *Left* — the Decision List, showing all pending decisions matching the active filters.
* *Center* — the Decision Area, showing the entity mention being resolved and the top candidate cluster proposed by the engine.
* *Top* — a bar for filtering and ordering the Decision List.

=== Decision List

The left panel lists all decisions matching the active filters.
Each item shows the entity name, its confidence and similarity scores (colour-coded), and when the decision was created.

Select any item to load its detail in the right panel.

image::curation/decision-list.png[Decision list with score badges,50%]

Scores are colour-coded so you can judge them at a glance:

[cols="1,1,2",options="header"]
|===
| Range | Colour | Reading

| 0.00 – 0.39 (Low)
| Red
| The engine is least certain — review carefully.

| 0.40 – 0.69 (Medium)
| Orange/yellow
| Moderate certainty.

| 0.70 – 1.00 (High)
| Green
| The engine is most certain.
|===


=== Filtering and Ordering Bar

The filter bar lets you narrow down the decision list by score range, full-text search, and sort order.
All filters can be combined and take effect instantly.

image::curation/filter-bar.png[Filter bar with Confidence and Similarity dropdowns]

[cols="1,3"]
|===
| Filter | Description

| *Entity Type*
| Restrict the list to decisions for a specific entity type.
Select one or more types from the dropdown, or leave it empty to show decisions for all entity types.

| *Confidence*
| Restrict by confidence score range.
Select a predefined band (Low / Medium / High) or set custom min/max values.
By default the list shows all pending decisions; use this filter to focus on the score range most in need of review.

| *Similarity*
| Restrict by similarity score range.
Select a predefined band or set custom min/max values.

| *Sort by*
| Order the decision list.
Options: _Created At (Newest/Oldest)_, _Updated At (Newest/Oldest)_, _Confidence (Low to High / High to Low)_.

| *Search*
| Full-text search across entity mention field data (name, identifiers, and other parsed attributes).

|===

=== Confirmation Preferences

The user pop-up provides toggles that suppress the confirmation dialog for individual actions — one each for *Accept*, *Reject*, and *Assign* — plus a single toggle that disables all confirmations at once.
When a toggle is on, clicking the action button (or pressing its keyboard shortcut) applies the action immediately, without a confirmation step.

image::curation/skip-confirmation-toggles.png[Skip-confirmation toggles,90%]

You can also set a preference during an action by checking the *Don't show again* box in the confirmation dialog.

image::curation/confirmation-dialog.png[Skip-confirmation in confirmation dialog,80%]

These preferences are only active during the current session.

[WARNING]
====
Curatorial actions are final and cannot be undone. Confirmation is enabled by default to provide a safety check before feedback is recorded. Turn it off only if you are confident, and use the option with caution.
====


=== Decision Area

The Decision Area displays the full details of a selected decision: the entity mention, the proposed cluster match with visual comparison aids, and a list of alternative candidates.
It is also where the curator records their feedback.

image::curation/decision-area.png[Decision area with comparison panel and alternative clusters]

[#alternative-clusters]
=== Alternative Clusters

Below the comparison cards, the page lists the other candidate clusters that the engine considered — ranked from best to worst after the top proposal.

Expand any cluster to compare its entities against the current entity mention, the same way as the proposed match.

image::curation/alternative-clusters.png[Alternative clusters section with expanded panel]


== Actions

This section explains the curation actions and how to perform each one in the interface.

[WARNING]
====
Curatorial actions are final and cannot be undone.
Past actions remain visible in the xref:UserGuide/curation-guide-history.adoc[Action History].
====

NOTE: Your feedback is recorded as a recommendation and forwarded to the engine for re-evaluation. The engine remains the authority for the resulting cluster assignment, which may be updated asynchronously after the recommendation is processed. This applies to every action below.

=== Reviewing a Decision

Reviewing the proposed match is the first step before you accept, reject, or select an alternative.

To review a decision:

. Select a decision from the Decision List. The comparison view opens in the Decision Area.
. Read the two cards shown side by side:
+
* *Left* — the entity mention being resolved, with its field values.
* *Right* — an entity of the proposed cluster, with the fields that differ from the entity mention highlighted.
. Use the *Previous* / *Next* controls on the right card to browse the other entities in the proposed cluster.
. Check the confidence (*C:*) and similarity (*S:*) scores shown in the header area.
. Decide whether the entity mention and the proposed cluster are the same real-world entity.

image::curation/comparison-panel.png[Comparison panel with entity cards and diff highlighting]

The highlight colours show *where each field value appears* — they do not represent edits or a before/after history. There are three cases:

[cols="1,1,2",options="header"]
|===
| Highlight | Meaning | Reading

| Green
| Only in the cluster
| The field value is present in the cluster entity but not in the entity mention.

| Red
| Only in the mention
| The field value is present in the entity mention but not in the cluster entity.

| Yellow
| Different in each
| The field is present in both, but the values differ.
|===

=== Accepting a Decision

Accepting a decision registers your feedback that the proposed cluster is a correct match for this entity mention.

. Select the decision in the list.
. Click the green check (*Accept*) button, *or* press kbd:[A].
. Confirm the action in the dialog that appears.
. The decision is removed from the queue and your feedback is recorded in the action history.

=== Rejecting a Decision

Rejecting a decision registers your feedback that none of the proposed candidates is an acceptable match.

. Select the decision in the list.
. Click the red cross (*Reject*) button, *or* press kbd:[R].
. Confirm the action in the dialog that appears.
. The decision is removed from the queue and your feedback is recorded in the action history.

=== Selecting an Alternative Cluster

Access the alternative clusters in the lower portion of the Decision Area as described in <<alternative-clusters,Alternative Clusters>>.

If one of the alternatives is a better match than the top proposal:

. Expand the cluster and inspect its entities.
. Click *Use this cluster instead*.
. Confirm the action in the dialog that appears.
. The decision is removed from the queue and your feedback is recorded in the action history.

Click *Load More* to fetch additional alternative clusters beyond those initially shown.

== Routine States

The following are normal situations, not errors. They are described generically because the exact on-screen wording is not covered here.

* *Empty queue* — if no decisions match the active filters, the Decision List is empty. Clear or widen the filters; if it is still empty, there is nothing pending to review right now.
* *An action does not take effect* — if recording feedback does not succeed, the decision may no longer be pending (for example, another curator reviewed the same decision first). Refresh the list and move on to the next decision.
* *Concurrent edits* — more than one curator can browse the queue at the same time. A decision already reviewed by someone else will not stay available for you to act on. Work from the current list and skip any decision that is no longer pending.

See also xref:UserGuide/curation-guide-overview.adoc#troubleshooting[Troubleshooting] in the overview.
