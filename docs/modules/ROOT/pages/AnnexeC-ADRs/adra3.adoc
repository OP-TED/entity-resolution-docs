== ADR-A3N — Identifier Stability and Assignment Mutability

=== Context and Problem Statement

ClusterIds are exposed as canonical identifiers and may be referenced by clients and downstream systems over long periods of time. +
At the same time, cluster assignment for a mention may change due to improved resolution logic, user recommendations, or reclustering. +
The architecture must protect identifier integrity and long-lived references while allowing assignment to evolve under ERE authority. +
A clear distinction is therefore required between:

* Stability of identifier existence.
* Mutability of mention-to-cluster assignment.

=== Decision

. *Identifier Non-Revocation*
.. A clusterId, once created, shall not be revoked, deleted, or reassigned to represent a different logical cluster.
.. ClusterId is never recycled i.e. identifier nont reused for another cluster.
.. ClusterId remains reserved even if the cluster becomes empty.
. *Assignment Mutability*
.. The assignment of a mention to a clusterId may change over time as determined by ERE.
.. A canonical identifier previously returned for a mention may therefore be replaced by a different clusterId in subsequent projections or delta responses.
. *Stability Definition*
.. Identity stability is defined at the level of identifier existence and non-reuse, not at the level of mention-to-cluster binding.
.. Stability does not imply immutability of assignment.
. *No Versioned Canonical Identifiers*
.. The system does not introduce versioned canonical identifiers, lineage chains, or revocation markers.
.. The current canonical identity of a mention is always the latest cluster assignment returned by ERE.

=== Scope and Applicability

This decision applies to all clusterIds exposed through ERS APIs and referenced by clients or downstream systems. +
It governs resolution, re-resolution, reclustering, and user-driven reassignment scenarios. +
Internal optimisation, storage compaction, or clustering strategy changes are out of scope as long as identifier non-reuse is preserved.

=== Considered Options

* Allow unstable identifiers with free revocation or invalidation of clusterIds.
* Introduce versioned or lineage-based canonical identifiers.
* Preserve identifier existence while allowing assignment mutability. _(Selected)_

=== Rationale

Preserving identifier existence protects long-lived references and avoids cascading invalidation across dependent systems. +
Allowing assignment mutability enables continuous improvement of clustering quality without requiring identifier migration or revocation semantics. +
Avoiding versioned or lineage-based identifiers keeps the external contract simple and prevents scope expansion into historical modelling.

=== Consequences

* ClusterIds may exist that are no longer assigned to a given mention.
* Clients must treat canonical identifiers as stable references, not as immutable indicators of cluster membership for a specific mention.
* Resolution improvements affect assignment, not identifier existence.
* Introducing revocation, reassignment of identifier values, or lineage exposure would require a new architectural decision.
